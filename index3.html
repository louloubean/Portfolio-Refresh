Project Presence

Description
Project Presence is a service concept for phone-free, analog skill workshops that help overstimulated people reclaim focus, learn tangible skills, and reconnect with others in real life.

Discover

01 — Problem Definition

Always-on screens are eroding focus, sleep, and real-world connection.

We framed the challenge as a human performance issue: How might we design phone-free experiences that help people feel present again?

Problem Framing

02 — Market Research & Competitive Landscape

Adjacent markets—from wellness retreats to craft education—offer escape, not lasting behavior change.

We identified whitespace for a recurring, community-driven model focused on hands-on skill development and phone-free presence.

Market Research Market Sizing

03 — User Insights & Personas

Based on user interviews, two primary personas emerged.

The Guides — skilled craftspeople looking for revenue, community, and a way to preserve their craft.

The Makers — overstimulated professionals seeking improved focus, creativity, and well-being.

Interview Insights

Define

04 — Ideation & Strategic Prioritization

We generated multiple service concepts and scored them against revenue potential, operational feasibility, differentiation, and speed to market.

A weighted prioritization model highlighted a community-focused, phone-free workshop ecosystem as the strongest path to repeat participation and predictable revenue.

Feature Prioritization

05 — Concept Definition

Project Presence is a recurring, in-person workshop model that restores attention and human connection through hands-on learning in phone-free environments.

Positioning: “Busy hands. Better focus. Real connection.”

Definition

06 — Unique Value Proposition

For individuals experiencing digital overload, Project Presence offers structured, phone-free skill-building environments that improve focus, well-being, and real-world connection.

Unlike single-event retreat models, it’s designed to be repeatable, community-driven, and integrated into everyday life.

09 — Next Steps

To validate Project Presence as a viable service model, additional work would focus on deeper user testing, operational modeling, and impact measurement.

  • 📍Pilot a small set of phone-free workshops with real participants
  • 📐Improve demand, pricing, and instructor utilization assumptions
  • 🧪Test presence and well-being outcomes with subject-matter experts
  • 🤝Explore partnerships with makerspaces, retreat centers, or guild networks
  • 📚Develop a Guide certification and curriculum framework
